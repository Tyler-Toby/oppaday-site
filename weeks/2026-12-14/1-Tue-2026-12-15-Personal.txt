The same email can get ignored in March and answered in May.
⠀
Nothing changed in the message. Something changed at the company.
⠀
That's the idea behind signal-based outbound: reaching out when there's a visible reason the timing might be right.
⠀
A few signals I pay attention to:
⠀
• They're hiring for a role connected to the problem you solve.
• They've raised funding and are about to grow.
• A new leader has joined and is setting priorities.
• They've adopted or changed a technology related to your offer.
⠀
A signal doesn't guarantee interest. It gives you a better reason to write, and a better first line.
