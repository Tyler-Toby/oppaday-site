Finding a signal is only half the work.
⠀
The other half is using it well in the email.
⠀
A structure I like:
⠀
1. The signal you noticed, in one sentence.
2. Why that moment often creates a specific need.
3. How you help, in plain terms.
4. One easy question.
⠀
And one rule: don't overstate what you know. A hiring post suggests a need. It doesn't prove one.
⠀
"Often when teams grow quickly..." lands better than "You must be struggling with..."
⠀
