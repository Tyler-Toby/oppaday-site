A generic list tells you who could buy. A signal suggests who might be ready to.
⠀
We use signals as a starting point for more relevant outreach:
⠀
1. Hiring: a company adding roles connected to the problem.
2. Funding: new money usually means new priorities.
3. Leadership changes: new leaders often review how things are done.
4. Technology changes: new tools can create new needs.
⠀
The signal shapes the message too. "I saw you're hiring three SDRs" opens a very different conversation than a generic introduction.
