Cold email or LinkedIn?
⠀
I get asked that a lot. My answer is usually both, for different jobs.
⠀
Cold email wins on scale and cost. With the right setup, you can reach far more people per day and run multi-step sequences.
⠀
LinkedIn often gets higher reply rates per message, especially with senior decision-makers, but on much smaller volume.
⠀
So I use email as the volume layer, and LinkedIn as a higher-touch layer for priority accounts.
