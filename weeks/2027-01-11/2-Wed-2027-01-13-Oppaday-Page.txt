We often get asked which channel works better for B2B outbound: cold email or LinkedIn.
⠀
Each wins in a different way:
⠀
• Cold email: scales to hundreds of contacts a day, low cost per contact, supports sequences and testing.
• LinkedIn: higher reply rates on smaller volumes, better for senior decision-makers and warm signals.
⠀
In head-to-head tests, LinkedIn often leads on reply rate. Cold email usually produces more total meetings because of volume.
⠀
Most B2B teams get the best results using both inside one repeatable process.
