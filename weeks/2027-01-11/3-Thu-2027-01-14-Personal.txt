Cold email and LinkedIn work best when each one does its own job.
⠀
Here's the simple weekly routine I'd use:
⠀
• Monday: refresh the email list and pick this week's LinkedIn priority accounts.
• Daily: let the email sequence run, and send a handful of personal connection requests.
• Friday: review replies on both channels and move warm conversations to a call.
⠀
Two rules: never send the same message on both channels at once, and keep one list of who was contacted where.
