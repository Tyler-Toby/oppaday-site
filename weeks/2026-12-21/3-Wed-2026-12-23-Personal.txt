AI can draft a cold email in seconds.
⠀
Reviewing it well takes longer, and that's where most of the value is.
⠀
My checklist for the human edit:
⠀
1. Check every fact. AI can be confidently wrong.
2. Check the fit for this audience.
3. Cut the generic phrases.
4. Trim the length and keep one easy ask.
5. Read it out loud.
6. Spot-check personalized fields before launch.
⠀
A wrong personal detail is worse than none at all.
⠀
