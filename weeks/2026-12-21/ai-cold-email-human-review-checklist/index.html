Reviewing AI-Written Cold Emails: A Checklist for the Human Edit

What to check before an AI-drafted cold email goes out, so it stays accurate, relevant and in your voice.

By Oppaday · Published November 17, 2026 · 2 min read

AI can draft a cold email in seconds. Reviewing it well takes a little longer, and that review is where most of the value is.

Here’s a checklist for the human edit, so AI-drafted emails stay accurate, relevant and recognizably yours.

Check every fact

AI can state things confidently that aren’t true: a company detail, a statistic, a claim about your own service. Verify anything specific before it goes out, or remove it.

Check the fit for this audience

Does the message address a problem this segment actually has? A well-written email to the wrong audience still won’t get replies. Make sure the draft matches the list it’s going to.

Cut the generic phrases

Watch for lines that could appear in anyone’s email: “I hope this finds you well,” “in today’s fast-paced world,” “best-in-class solutions.” Replace them with something specific, or delete them.

Check the length and the ask

AI drafts tend to run long. Trim to the essentials and make sure there’s one clear, easy question at the end, not two or three asks.

Read it out loud

If it doesn’t sound like something you’d say to a person, rewrite it until it does. Your voice is part of why people reply.

Personalize carefully

Personalization at scale is useful, but a wrong detail is worse than none. Spot-check personalized fields across the list before launching.
