AI can write a cold email in seconds.
⠀
That's the easy part.
⠀
The hard part is still deciding who should get it, what problem it should address, and whether it sounds like something a real person would send.
⠀
How I think about AI in cold email:
⠀
• Use it to draft first lines, suggest subject lines, and clean up contact data.
• Use it to segment lists so the message fits the audience.
• Have a person review before anything goes out.
⠀
Fully AI-generated emails tend to sound generic, and buyers notice. Human-edited AI drafts are what land.
