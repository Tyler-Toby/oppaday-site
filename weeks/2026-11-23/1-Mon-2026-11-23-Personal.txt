A new sending domain can do everything right on day one and still land in spam.
⠀
Usually not because of the message.
⠀
Because the domain hasn't earned any trust yet.
⠀
Before a cold email campaign goes live, I want three things in place:
⠀
• Authentication set up: SPF, DKIM and DMARC.
• A clear, easy way for people to opt out.
• A warm-up period, with sending volume increased gradually instead of all at once.
⠀
None of that is exciting. All of it decides whether the message gets seen.
⠀
The mailbox providers have tightened their rules over the last couple of years. Skipping setup costs more than it used to.
