When a cold email campaign underperforms, the first question we ask isn't about the copy.
⠀
It's whether the email is reaching the inbox at all.
⠀
Before any campaign launches, we check the sending setup:
⠀
1. SPF, DKIM and DMARC are configured for the sending domain.
2. Every email includes a simple way to opt out.
3. New domains are warmed up before campaign volume starts.
4. Daily sending increases gradually, not overnight.
⠀
A strong message on a domain that isn't ready can quietly end up in spam. Nobody replies, and it looks like the message failed.
⠀
Setup first. Then volume. Then judge the message.
