Before a new sending domain carries a real campaign, I run through seven checks.
⠀
1. A separate domain for outreach.
2. SPF, DKIM and DMARC set up and passing.
3. A warm-up period before campaign volume.
4. Daily sending increased in steps.
5. An easy way to opt out in every email.
6. A verified list, so bounces stay low.
7. A close watch on the first week of results.
⠀
Most of these take minutes.
⠀
Skipping them can cost weeks of quiet results that look like a message problem, when the real problem is that nobody saw the message.
