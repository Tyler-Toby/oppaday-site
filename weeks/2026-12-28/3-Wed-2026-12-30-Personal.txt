January is a good time to restart outbound, not just resume it.
⠀
Five things I'd do in the first weeks of the year:
⠀
1. Review what actually produced conversations last year.
2. Refresh and re-verify the contact list.
3. Pick one focus for the first quarter.
4. Check the sending setup before volume picks back up.
5. Set a simple weekly and monthly review rhythm.
⠀
None of it is complicated. Doing it before the first campaign goes out is what makes the difference.
