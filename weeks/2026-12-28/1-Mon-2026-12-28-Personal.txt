Before planning next year's outbound, I start with step one: the contact list.
⠀
Not the message. Not the tools. The list.
⠀
Because a year's worth of campaigns built on the wrong list is a year of wasted effort.
⠀
Going into the new year, I'm asking:
⠀
• Which companies actually turned into good conversations this year?
• Which roles replied, and which never did?
• Which filters should be tightened?
• How much of the list needs to be re-verified?
⠀
The answers shape everything that follows.
