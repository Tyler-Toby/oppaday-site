The first step of every outbound process we run is the same: build the right contact list.
⠀
As the year wraps up, it's a good time to revisit yours:
⠀
1. Targeting: are the filters still aimed at the companies that fit best?
2. Contacts: are you reaching the people who own the problem?
3. Verification: how much of the list has gone stale?
⠀
People change jobs. Companies grow, shrink and change direction. A list that was right in January can be out of date by December.
⠀
A fresh, verified list is the best foundation for next year's campaigns.
