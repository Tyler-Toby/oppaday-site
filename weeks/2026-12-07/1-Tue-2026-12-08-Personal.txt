A lot of replies to cold email don't come from the first message.
⠀
People are busy. The first email arrives on the wrong day, gets skimmed, and slides down the inbox.
⠀
That's why follow-ups matter.
⠀
But more isn't automatically better. Every extra email is another chance to annoy someone who isn't interested.
⠀
The questions I ask when planning a sequence:
⠀
• How many touches does this audience need to notice us?
• How much space between them?
• Does each follow-up add something, or just repeat the first email?
⠀
