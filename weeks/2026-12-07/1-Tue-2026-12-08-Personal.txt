Most replies to a cold email sequence come from the first or second message.
⠀
That surprises people who send seven or eight follow-ups.
⠀
Emails three and four can still reach people who missed the first ones, if they stay short and relevant. Past five or six, the extra volume rarely pays for itself, and it adds deliverability risk.
⠀
The questions I ask when planning a sequence:
⠀
• How many touches does this audience need to notice us?
• How much space between them?
• Does each follow-up add something, or just repeat the first email?
⠀
For most B2B programs, three or four touches spaced a few days apart is the practical sweet spot.
