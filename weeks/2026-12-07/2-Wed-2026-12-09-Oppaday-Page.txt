We plan follow-ups before the first cold email goes out, not after it gets no reply.
⠀
When we build a sequence, we decide three things up front:
⠀
1. Length: usually three or four touches.
2. Spacing: two to four days apart, so it feels human.
3. Purpose: what each follow-up adds that the first email didn't.
⠀
Most of the value comes from the first two emails. Each extra touch adds volume, so we only add follow-ups when the data shows they still produce replies.
⠀
The goal is to be noticed by the people who care and to stop before you become noise to the ones who don't.
