Most follow-ups remind people you exist. Very few give them a new reason to reply.
⠀
What I try to do instead:
⠀
• Give every follow-up a job: a new angle, a short example, or a simpler question.
• Keep each one shorter than the last.
• Space them out.
• Decide in advance when to stop, and close the loop politely.
⠀
