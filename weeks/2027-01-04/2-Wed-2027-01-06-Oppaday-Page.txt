When we review an outbound campaign, we don't stop at activity metrics.
⠀
We look at the whole path from send to sale:
⠀
1. Bounce rate: are emails being delivered?
2. Unique reply rate: are people responding?
3. Positive reply rate: is there real interest?
4. Meetings held: does interest turn into conversations?
5. Qualified opportunities: is it creating pipeline that fits?
⠀
Each number points to a different fix. Delivery problems need list and setup work. Few relevant replies usually mean the audience and offer don't fit yet. Meetings without qualified opportunities point back to targeting.
