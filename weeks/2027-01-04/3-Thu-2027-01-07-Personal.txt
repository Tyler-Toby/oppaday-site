Once a month, I sit down with the outbound numbers and ask six questions, in order:
⠀
1. Are the emails being delivered?
2. Who is replying?
3. How many replies show real interest?
4. Is interest turning into meetings?
5. Are the meetings the right meetings?
6. What's the one thing to change next month?
⠀
The order matters. There's no point rewriting the message if the emails aren't arriving.
⠀
And one change at a time, so you can tell what worked.
