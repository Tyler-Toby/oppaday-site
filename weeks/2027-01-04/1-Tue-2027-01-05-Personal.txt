Open rates used to be the first number everyone asked about.
⠀
I don't lean on them anymore.
⠀
Privacy features in some email apps load images automatically, so an "open" doesn't always mean a person read the email.
⠀
The numbers I watch instead:
⠀
• Bounce rate, to catch delivery problems.
• Unique reply rate: how many people respond.
• Positive reply rate: how many show real interest.
• Meetings held and qualified opportunities.
⠀
A high reply rate full of "not interested" isn't a win. A modest one that turns into qualified conversations is.
