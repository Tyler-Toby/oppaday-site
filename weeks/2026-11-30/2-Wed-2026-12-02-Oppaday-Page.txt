Every cold email campaign we run starts with the same question: who exactly are we writing to?
⠀
Here's how we build a B2B contact list:
⠀
1. Define the accounts: the industries, company sizes and locations that fit the offer.
2. Choose the roles: the people responsible for the problem the offer solves.
3. Filter out the noise: wrong titles, wrong company types, and contacts already reached.
4. Verify the emails before anything is sent.
⠀
The list decides who hears the message. Getting it right makes every other part of the campaign easier.
