Most cold email problems start before anyone writes the email.
⠀
They start with the list.
⠀
A list built in a hurry usually has the same issues: companies that don't fit, titles that don't own the problem, and addresses that bounce.
⠀
When I build a list, I go in this order:
⠀
• Pick the accounts first: industry, size, location, and why they'd care.
• Then choose the people inside them who own the problem you solve.
• Then verify every email address before it goes anywhere near a campaign.
⠀
A smaller list of the right people beats a big list of almost-right ones.
